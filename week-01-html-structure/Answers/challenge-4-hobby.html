Why badminton?

Badminton is fast, cheap to start, and you can play it all year in Singapore.

Things I need

  • A racket
  • Shuttlecocks
  • A court booking

Basic rules

How a point is won

  1. The server hits the shuttle diagonally across the net.
  2. Players rally until the shuttle lands on the floor.
  3. The side that wins the rally scores a point.

First to 21 points (win by 2) wins the game. Last updated 1 November 2026.

Tip: warm up your shoulders before every game.

Glossary

Smash
A powerful overhead shot hit downwards.
Clear
A high shot that lands at the back of the opponent's court.
Drop shot
A gentle shot that just clears the net.
